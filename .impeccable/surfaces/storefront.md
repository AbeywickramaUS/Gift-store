# Surface brief — Storefront (all customer pages)

Scope: home, collections (Skincare/Glow, Accessories/Charms, Gift Sets/Moments, Minis), product, gift set, Our Story, Order Help, bag, checkout, confirmation, 404.
Mode: Persuade (home, collections, product, story) · Read (Order Help) · Operate (bag, checkout).
Audience & job: phone-first visitors from Instagram/TikTok, half self-treat, half gift buyers. Find a treat or a ready gift set, add it to the bag, and place a WhatsApp order with confidence before a bank transfer.
Proof on hand: the real catalogue (names, prices, stock) and the GIFMIRA brand kit. No product photos, reviews or numbers yet: never invent them.

History: Sachet Shelf, then Sticker Shop, replaced by the owner's GIFMIRA brand ("elegant premium look").

## Direction contract

THESIS: GIFMIRA is a gift boutique: every page is unwrapped like a present from a premium shop. A blush satin ribbon tied in the GIFMIRA bow runs through the site, and products sit in arched boutique-window frames. Refuses the category default of a white grid of product photos with a sale banner.

OWN-WORLD: Cream #FFF6EF paper, burgundy #7A1F3D Playfair Display headings with italic rose accents, Poppins body in dark ink #3A1A27, blush #F7B6C8 satin ribbon and window frames, champagne #D4AF7F hairlines, foil sparkles and knot. The illustrations are re-toned to the brand palette (burgundy outline, blush/rose/champagne/cream fills). Burgundy pill buttons.

STORY: The visitor feels a thoughtful little boutique, sees curated treats and wrapped sets at accessible prices, understands the WhatsApp-plus-bank-transfer ordering at a glance, adds to the bag, and checks out.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim burgundy service line (delivery, WhatsApp, no COD). The header has the GIFMIRA horizontal logo and the brand menu. The hero: H1 "Little Gifts, Big Smiles." in Playfair (the second phrase italic, in rose), the brand's opening paragraph, burgundy "Shop gift sets" and an outline "Explore skincare". On the right, a large blush gift box wrapped with a satin ribbon and the GIFMIRA bow, with three product windows (arched frames) peeking out. Then the collections row: Glow · Charms · Moments · Minis.

FORM: Pinned by the GIFMIRA brand kit (palette, type, logo, collections and menu are all specified by the owner). seed: none (pinned): "use the GIFMIRA brand kit … elegant premium look".

Signature interaction: an arched product window lifts slightly and its ribbon tag swings into view on hover/focus; adding to the bag ties a small bow animation on the bag icon.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
